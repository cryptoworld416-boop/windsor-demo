Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What event décor options are available?+

Windsor Party Rentals offers a wide collection of modern event décor, including: Backdrops (velvet drapes, custom arch walls, and floral walls); Lighting & Accent (LED marquee letters, candelabras, and ambient stage lighting); Tables & Seating (serpentine head tables, cocktail tables, and stretch spandex covers); Table Decor (centerpieces, charger plates, and specialty linens).

Are your décor items fully insured and approved for commercial halls?+

Yes. Windsor Party Rentals operates as a fully insured décor rental company. Our team complies with all venue safety requirements across Windsor-Essex commercial halls, carrying general liability insurance to ensure seamless setup and teardown for large-scale weddings, corporate functions, and private galas.

Are delivery, setup, and pickup fees included?+

Prices are not inclusive of delivery, setup, or pickup fees. These are quoted separately based on your venue and requirements.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Windsor-Essex and surrounding communities, including Windsor, LaSalle, Tecumseh, Amherstburg, Lakeshore, Kingsville, and Leamington.

Do you welcome custom visions?+

Yes. Don't see exactly what you're looking for? We love a creative challenge. Our team specializes in bespoke event styling and can help curate a package that fits your unique theme and budget perfectly.
